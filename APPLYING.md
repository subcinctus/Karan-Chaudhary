# Applying with this site

This is your guide to using the site for film-school applications and for people hiring a DP.

- **Sources.** Every fact below about a school or about hiring comes from a research sweep made in
  October 2026. Each one links to its source and says when it was checked. Requirements change every
  year, so open the school's own page again before you submit.
- **Weaker sources are flagged.** Where a page could not be read directly (a search excerpt, a
  third-party agency, an old document), the line says so.
- **Everything else is about this site:** what to fill in, where it shows, how to export.

**What this means for your submissions**

- **Photographs are not frames.** AFI's photograph PDF excludes "set stills or frames from a motion
  picture" ([AFI](https://conservatory.afi.com/admissions-requirements/), checked 2026-10). Put your
  own still photography in `photographs` in `data/portfolio.js`, never a frame grab. The data check
  fails a photograph that is also one of a film's frames, and warns when it is a behind-the-scenes still.
- **Be credited for the right jobs.** NFTS wants to see you credited "as the camera operator and also
  the creator of the lighting design" ([NFTS](https://www.nfts.co.uk/cinematography), checked 2026-10).
  Fill in `operated` and `lit` for each film, and the real names in `crew`.
- **Say how each film was made.** AFI asks, for each film, its format and camera, whether it was made at
  a school, and any AI use ([AFI](https://conservatory.afi.com/admissions-requirements/),
  checked 2026-10). Fill in `madeAt` and `aiUse`, and the camera and lens fields you already have.
- **Leave the code-rendered work out where AI is not allowed.** USC allows no AI in any written or media
  portion ([USC](https://cinema.usc.edu/admissions/procedures/production/graduateprocedures.cfm),
  checked 2026-10), and Columbia does not accept AI-generated creative work
  ([Columbia](https://arts.columbia.edu/admissions/graduate), search excerpt, checked 2026-10).
  - The studies, the main title and the previs plans on this site are code written with AI assistance,
    so keep them out of those submissions.
  - Where AI use must be disclosed (AFI, NYU, sources above), say so plainly. Section 6 explains how.

## 2. What people hiring a DP say

- **Reel length: about 1–2½ minutes, never more than 3.**
  - "60 to 90 seconds is the modern standard", 2 minutes at most ([Boldcine](https://www.boldcine.com/university/lessons/showcasing-your-work-online), checked 2026-10).
  - "Two minutes is good"; "If you're running five, six minutes, no one's watching that long" (agent Dan Burnside, [Sony Cine](https://sony-cinematography.com/articles/reels-for-cinematographers-an-agent-s-perspective/), checked 2026-10).
  - For a less-established DP, "maybe two, two-and-a-half minutes" (studio executive Glenn Gainor, [Sony Cine](https://sony-cinematography.com/articles/reels-for-cinematographers-part-2-a-studio-exec-s-perspective/), checked 2026-10).
  - "Three minutes or less" (Jay Holben, ASC, [CLAi](https://www.clai.tv/the-cinematographers-reel/), checked 2026-10). AFI's limit is also 3 minutes ([AFI](https://conservatory.afi.com/admissions-requirements/), checked 2026-10).
  - On this site, the data check fails a reel over 3:00 and warns outside 1:00–2:30.
- **Lead with your best shot.**
  - "The first 5 seconds are non-negotiable" ([Boldcine](https://www.boldcine.com/university/lessons/showcasing-your-work-online), checked 2026-10).
  - "Why did you bury the lede? Why is this third?" (Burnside, [Sony Cine](https://sony-cinematography.com/articles/reels-for-cinematographers-an-agent-s-perspective/), checked 2026-10).
  - A creative director may click off after "20 to 25 seconds" ([CLAi](https://www.clai.tv/the-cinematographers-reel/), checked 2026-10).
  - Don't spend "the first twenty seconds putting your name on screen" (Rubidium Wu, [PremiumBeat](https://www.premiumbeat.com/blog/reasons-not-to-cut-your-own-dp-showreel/), checked 2026-10).
- **Vimeo first.**
  - NFTS takes up to 3 Vimeo links ([NFTS](https://www.nfts.co.uk/cinematography), checked 2026-10), so your films need to be there anyway.
  - Vimeo's free plan (announced 7 Oct 2026) includes 3 GB for embeddable or non-public videos, no ads, and 1 TB of bandwidth a month. It is not available in Italy or Austria ([Vimeo blog](https://vimeo.com/blog/post/new-free-plan), [Vimeo help](https://help.vimeo.com/hc/en-us/articles/12425432518801-What-s-included-with-the-Free-plan), checked 2026-10).
  - Section 5 explains how to put the reel on the site.
- **Show a scene, not a montage.**
  - "Many reels have a montage of beautiful shots, but virtually none from the same scene" (producer Royce Dudley, [Stage 32](https://www.stage32.com/lounge/cinematography/How-Producers-Look-Cinematography-Reels-When-Considering-DPs), checked 2026-10).
  - "You will want to see complete scenes and how they are covered and cut" ([Stage 32](https://stage32.com/post/1039221930328072295), checked 2026-10).
  - Include "a few whole sequences" (Steven Bernstein, ASC, [Sony Cine](https://sony-cinematography.com/articles/cinematographer-reels---a-director-s-perspective/), checked 2026-10).
  - A director hiring a DP may ask you to "walk me through a scene from your reel and tell me what you would change" ([Aaron Lieber](https://aaronlieber.com/blog/how-to-hire-a-dp/), checked 2026-10).
  - On this site, a film's `sequences` show one scene in 3–8 frames, with your intent for each and an "In hindsight" line.
- **Make your role clear.**
  - Producers check "that ghostly handheld was them and not their operator" ([Stage 32](https://www.stage32.com/lounge/cinematography/How-Producers-Look-Cinematography-Reels-When-Considering-DPs), checked 2026-10).
  - "Be clear on what the DP actually did in the reel" ([Stage 32](https://stage32.com/post/1039221930328072295), checked 2026-10).
  - Label the films clearly and credit the director (Bernstein, [Sony Cine](https://sony-cinematography.com/articles/cinematographer-reels---a-director-s-perspective/), checked 2026-10).
  - On this site, the film page's credits read from `role`, `operated`, `lit` and `crew`.
- **Keep the site simple and fast.**
  - "If it's too fancy, if you put too much attention on the actual website, then it's suspect" (Gainor, [Sony Cine](https://sony-cinematography.com/articles/reels-for-cinematographers-part-2-a-studio-exec-s-perspective/), checked 2026-10).
  - "Simplicity is the order of the day" (Bernstein, [Sony Cine](https://sony-cinematography.com/articles/cinematographer-reels---a-director-s-perspective/), checked 2026-10).
  - "If your homepage takes more than 3 seconds, producers leave", and give "an actual working email — not a contact form" ([Boldcine](https://www.boldcine.com/university/lessons/showcasing-your-work-online), checked 2026-10).
  - On this site, a Contact button leads to your email from every page (in the header, the bar of a study page, and on the 404), and the "Motion off" button stops every animation.
- **Mind the sound.**
  - For music, "you're always better off using something that's not recognizable" (Burnside, [Sony Cine](https://sony-cinematography.com/articles/reels-for-cinematographers-an-agent-s-perspective/), checked 2026-10).
  - "Turn the sound off. Really." (Dudley, [Stage 32](https://stage32.com/post/1039221930328072295), checked 2026-10).
  - A music-only reel needs no captions, but tell viewers there is no important audio ([W3C WAI](https://www.w3.org/WAI/media/av/captions/), checked 2026-10). On this site, `reel.dialogue: false` adds "No dialogue — instrumental" under the reel.
- **Keep it current.**
  - "A 5-year-old reel signals you haven't worked recently"; "Mark work that is a year or more old" ([Boldcine](https://www.boldcine.com/university/lessons/showcasing-your-work-online), checked 2026-10).
  - On this site, set `reel.updated` and the reel reads "Reel 2026 · updated October 2026". The data check warns when it is over 12 months old.

## 4. Export the photographs PDF

1. **Add your photographs.** Add them to `photographs.series` in `data/portfolio.js`.
   - Give each one its `alt` text and its `w` and `h` in pixels.
   - Give each one the `sets` it belongs to: `afi`, `nyu`, `famu`, `lodz`, `bfa`.
   - Mark portraits of people with `portrait: true`.
2. **Use JPEG files.** On this site, Chrome copies a JPEG into the PDF unchanged and a PNG at about its own
   size, which for a photograph is several times a JPEG's; a WebP becomes far larger: in a test in October
   2026, a 16 KB WebP became 283 KB inside the PDF.
   - The PDF weighs about as much as the JPEGs in it together, and AFI's limit is 15 MB. The data check
     warns when one set's images add up to more than 13 MB.
   - FAMU wants JPGs of 2000 px or less
     ([FAMU](https://www.famu.cz/en/departments/famu-international-fi/one-year-courses/cinematography/),
     checked 2026-10).
3. **Check the counts.** Run `npm run check:static`. The data section says how many photographs each set
   holds and warns when a set does not fit the school.
4. **Open the set in Chrome.** Go to `photographs.html?set=afi` on the live site, or on your machine:
   `npm run serve`, then <http://127.0.0.1:8080/Karan-Chaudhary/photographs.html?set=afi>.
5. **Print.** Press **Print / Save as PDF**. It loads every photograph first, then opens the print dialog.
   - Destination: **Save as PDF**.
   - Paper: A4 or Letter.
   - Margins: **Default** (the page sets 12 mm).
   - Untick **Headers and footers**. Otherwise Chrome prints the date, the title and the address on
     every page, and AFI asks for no captions.
6. **Check the file.**
   - One photograph per page, no blank page, and the right number of pages.
   - 15 MB or less ([AFI](https://conservatory.afi.com/admissions-requirements/), checked 2026-10).
   - Chrome offers the page title as the file name ("Karan Chaudhary — Photographs, AFI set").

**Options you can add to the address:**
- `?captions=1` prints each photograph's `caption` under it. AFI asks for no captions, so leave it off
  for AFI.
- `?cover=1` puts a cover page first: your name, your contact and the list of series. AFI asks for the
  photographs, one per page, so leave the cover off unless a school asks for one.

Without a `?set=`, the page shows every photograph. While `photographs` is empty, the page says "No
photographs yet.", search engines are asked not to index it, and the home page has no Photographs section.

## 5. Put the reel (and the films) on Vimeo

- **Unlisted videos.** An unlisted Vimeo link looks like `https://vimeo.com/123456789/abcdef1234`.
  - The first number is the video's id, and the letters and digits after it are its hash.
  - Put both in the reel:
    `reel: { provider: 'vimeo', id: '123456789', hash: 'abcdef1234', … }`.
  - The site's player then asks Vimeo for that video with its hash, and the data check checks the hash's
    format.
- **Links for AFI.** AFI asks for links that are "NOT password protected", one film per link, unchanged
  after you submit and live until August 2027
  ([AFI](https://conservatory.afi.com/admissions-requirements/), checked 2026-10). Put each film's own
  link in its `watch`.
- **Label the reel.** Fill in `reel.updated` (the month of the last cut), `reel.runtime`,
  `reel.uploadDate` (`'YYYY-MM-DD'`) and a `reel.poster` (your own frame).
  - With a poster and an upload date, search engines get the reel's details and its key moments.
- **A shot index.** List your shots in `reel.shots`, in reel order: `{ t: '0:41', film: '<slug>',
  lens: '40 mm', note: 'one line', thumb: 'media/reel/shots/041.jpg' }`.
  - Each one becomes a tile that plays the reel from that moment.
  - `index.html?t=41#reel` opens the reel at 0:41 (the player starts when the visitor presses play).
  - Vimeo's own `#t=` links work the same way
    ([Vimeo help](https://help.vimeo.com/hc/en-us/articles/12425821012497-Start-playback-at-a-specific-timecode),
    checked 2026-10).
- **Captions.** For a reel with dialogue, set `reel.captions: { lang: 'en', label: 'English' }`.
  - On Vimeo and YouTube the site asks the player to show the captions you uploaded there in that
    language (`texttrack`; `cc_load_policy` and `cc_lang_pref`).
  - For a video file you host yourself, add `src` with a `.vtt` file.
  - Automatic captions only count once you have checked them for accuracy
    ([W3C WAI](https://www.w3.org/WAI/media/av/captions/), checked 2026-10).

## 6. Recorded clips of the studies

The Focus, Light and Colour studies, the main title and the previs plans are not footage. They are
pieces rendered from code written with AI assistance. On the site each study is labelled "Interactive
study · code written with AI assistance · not camera work", and the colophon says how the site was made.

- **Record.** `npm run record -- focus` (and so on) writes a clip to `.artifacts/clips/` (see the
  README).
- **Label it the same way.** When you send a clip, call it a code-rendered study in its title and
  description, never camera work.
- **Disclose AI where asked.** AFI wants AI use disclosed for each film and in a footnote to the
  statement ([AFI](https://conservatory.afi.com/admissions-requirements/), checked 2026-10). NYU asks
  for disclosure ([NYU](https://tisch.nyu.edu/grad-film/admissions/portfolio-requirements-for-mfa-in-filmmaking),
  search excerpt, checked 2026-10).
- **Leave them out where AI is not allowed.** Leave the clips out of USC and Columbia submissions
  (section 1).
- **Confirm the colophon.** Check that `colophon.text` matches how you worked, then set
  `colophon.confirmed: true`. Until then the site shows "To confirm" next to it.

## 7. Honesty rules

The site is built so that it can never say more than is true. Keep it that way.

- **Never invent anything.** No awards, festivals, crew names, references or AI-use statements that are
  not real and confirmed. Fill a field only with the truth, and leave it `null` or `[]` until then: an
  empty field simply does not show.
- **SAMPLE badges stay until the content is real.** Every record marked `placeholder: true` shows a
  visible **SAMPLE** tag.
  - Replace the content first, then set `placeholder: false`.
  - Turn off `site.showPlaceholderBadges` only when nothing is a placeholder any more.
- **No sample left inside a real film (the nested guard).** The sample lighting plot and the sample scene
  carry their own `placeholder: true` and "Sample" badges. When you mark a film real, replace those blocks
  or delete them: CI fails while a sample block sits inside a film that is not a placeholder, and the site
  does not deploy.
- **Laurels only on real films.** `premiere` and `awards` on a sample film fail the check.
- **Photographs are never samples.** The print view is made to be submitted, so the data check fails a
  photograph marked `placeholder: true` and any photograph that is also a film frame.
- **Test data never ships.** The checks render a fixture (`tools/test/fixtures/full.mjs`: "Crew Name A",
  "Fixture Festival", the site's own posters standing in for photographs) in the browser only. Its
