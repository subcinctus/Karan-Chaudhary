Shot language

Six camera moves, the grammar behind the cards in my selected work. Each one is drawn in the palette of a film and cropped to the aspect ratio it was delivered in, so the same move reads differently in a 2.76 coastline and a 4:3 documentary.

Hover or focus a frame: the move plays once and holds. Move away and it returns.

    Camera moves: Motion Prompt #027 #030 #031 #032 #033 #043 by Shohei Ohtaki (MIT), run unchanged and repainted. Vignette and grain from the numbers of #112 and #118.
