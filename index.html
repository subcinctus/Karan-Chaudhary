Sc. 04About & approach

About

I am a cinematographer. I light for the moment a scene turns, and I would rather move the camera once, for a reason, than often for effect.

Sample bio

On set I start from what the scene is about, then work out where the light would plausibly come from and how close the lens needs to be to the actors.

I like small crews, long takes and practical sources I can put in the frame. I plan with overhead diagrams and previs so the day itself can stay open to the actors.

The studies on this site are how I think about the job: depth of field as a choice about attention, key-to-fill ratio as a choice about mood, and the grade as the last step of the lighting.

Approach

  1. 01

    Motivated light

    Every source should have a reason to be where it is. I build the key from the practical or the window the audience can see, then decide how much of the face to give away.

    See it in the Light study · Rembrandt →

  2. 02

    Focus is attention

    Where the plane of focus sits tells the audience where to look. I choose the stop for the scene, not for the lens.

    See it in the Focus study →

  3. 03

    Colour carries the scene

    I light for the grade I intend: warm against cool, separation before saturation, highlights that roll off rather than clip.

    See it in the Colour study →
